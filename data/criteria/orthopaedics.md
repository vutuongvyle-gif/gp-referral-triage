# Orthopaedics referral criteria (SYNTHETIC)

## ORTHO-01 Hip or knee osteoarthritis
### Accept when
- Symptoms for at least 3 months despite first-line management
- Significant impact on daily activity, sleep or work is documented
### Required before referral
- Weight-bearing X-ray of the affected joint within the last 12 months
- Documented trial of analgesia and an exercise or physiotherapy programme
### Red flags (use the acute pathway instead)
- Hot, swollen joint with fever
- Suspected fracture after trauma
### Alternatives if criteria are not met
- Specialist advice request
- Community physiotherapy or joint education programme
